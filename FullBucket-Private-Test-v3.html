A quick note from Kenny

Why I built this.

I spent a lot of years taking care of responsibilities, people and work. I knew why I was doing it. The question I hadn't spent much time asking was: What about me?

I eventually went through a values exercise that helped me separate what I thought was supposed to matter from what actually mattered to me. But identifying a value was only the beginning. I still had to figure out what actually supported it — and whether there was room for any of that in my real life.

Full Bucket isn't here to tell you how to live. It asks three practical questions: What matters? What supports it? Are you making room for it?

A quick note from Kenny

This part shouldn't feel like homework.

Look, guys, I don't know if you're anything like me, but give me a blank weekly schedule and tell me to fill it out and there's a pretty good chance I'm closing the app.

I purposely designed this to make it as easy as possible. I'll ask you a few questions and build the shape of your life from your answers. You don't have to account for every minute. We just need to be realistic enough about where your time goes for this to be useful.

We're not trying to get all up in your life. Skip anything that doesn't apply. This setup is a one-time thing, and you can change it later.
